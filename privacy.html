Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-12. We take your privacy seriously and only use your information to provide the cash offer service you requested.

1. Who We Are

OneCashOffer (operated by WETYR Corp, "we", "us", "our") provides cash home buying services across the United States. We are based in Florida and serve all 50 states. You can contact us at info@onecashoffer.com or (321) 555-0199.

2. Information We Collect

When you submit a property inquiry through our forms, we collect:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address
  • Property information: address, city, condition, property type, timeframe
  • Optional message: any additional details you provide
  • Technical information: IP address, browser type, referring URL, UTM campaign parameters, the page you submitted the form from

We do not collect Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or other sensitive financial information through our website forms.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Prepare and deliver a cash offer for your property
  • Contact you by phone, email, or text to discuss your inquiry
  • Verify property details using public records and comparable sales data
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Send you transactional emails (offer confirmations, closing updates)

We do not sell your information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share your information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the business (title companies, real estate attorneys, our database provider Supabase, our email provider Resend, our hosting provider Cloudflare)
  • Closing partners if you accept our offer (the title company handling the transaction)
  • Government agencies when required by law (tax reporting, anti-money-laundering compliance)

If we cannot purchase your property directly, we may refer your inquiry to one of our trusted investor partners with your consent.

5. Communications (TCPA / Phone & Text)

By submitting our form, you consent to receive phone calls, text messages, and emails from OneCashOffer at the phone number and email address you provide, including communications using auto-dialed or pre-recorded voice messages, for the purpose of discussing the sale of your property. Standard message and data rates may apply. Your consent is not a condition of any purchase, and you can opt out at any time by replying STOP to texts or telling us by phone or email.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies for site functionality (form submissions, session state). We do not use third-party advertising cookies or sell tracking data. UTM parameters in URLs are captured to help us understand which marketing channels work — this data stays internal.

7. Data Retention

We retain your inquiry data for as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations (typically 7 years for closed transactions, 3 years for unclosed inquiries). You can request deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@onecashoffer.com.

8. Your Rights

Under applicable privacy laws (including CCPA for California residents, CDPA for Virginia, and similar state laws), you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you
  • Request a copy of your information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of your information (we do not sell)
  • Be free from discrimination for exercising these rights

To exercise these rights, email info@onecashoffer.com with your request.

9. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure database hosting (Supabase), and access controls. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact info@onecashoffer.com and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced on our homepage.
