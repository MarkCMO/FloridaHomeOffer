## WHAT WE DO

OneCashOffer purchases residential and commercial property directly from owners for cash. Sellers fill out a short form (or call), receive a written cash offer within 24 hours, and can close at a title company in 7 to 14 days. We charge zero fees, zero commissions, and require zero repairs.

Our core offerings:

- **Cash Offers on Single-Family Homes:** Free, no-obligation, written offer within 24 hours. We buy in any condition - move-in ready, needs repairs, fire/water/mold damage, hoarder condition, condemned, anything.
- **Cash Offers on Condos and Townhouses:** Including non-warrantable condos, condos with HOA litigation, condos with special assessments (very common post-Surfside in Florida).
- **Cash Offers on Multi-Family Properties:** Duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes, and larger apartment buildings. We buy with tenants in place - no eviction required.
- **Cash Offers on Commercial Real Estate:** Office buildings, retail storefronts, strip malls, medical offices, restaurants, auto repair shops. Occupied or vacant.
- **Cash Offers on Vacant Land:** Residential lots, commercial parcels, agricultural acreage, waterfront land, wooded tracts, landlocked parcels.
- **Cash Offers on Mobile and Manufactured Homes:** Single-wide, double-wide, modular. With or without the land. In parks or on private land.
- **Cash Offers on Industrial Property:** Warehouses, distribution centers, flex space, light industrial, storage facilities.
- **Cash Offers on Farms, Ranches, and Agricultural Land:** Working farms, cattle ranches, equestrian facilities, citrus groves, timber land.
- **Cash Offers on Mixed-Use Properties:** Retail with apartments above, office plus residential combos, live-work spaces.
- **Cash Offers on Distressed/Special-Situation Properties:** Foreclosure-pending, probate, divorce, inherited, tax-lien, code-violation, fire-damaged, hurricane-damaged, mold-damaged, foundation-issue, Chinese-drywall, unpermitted-work, problem-tenant, hoarder-house, vacant, and tired-landlord situations.

**We are NOT:**
- A real estate agent or brokerage. We do not list properties on MLS. We are a direct buyer.
- A franchise. OneCashOffer is wholly owned and operated by WETYR Corp.
- An iBuyer charging 5-8% service fees (like Opendoor or Offerpad). Our offers carry zero seller fees and zero closing costs.
- A wholesaler who flips contracts without intent to close. We close on every offer we extend that the seller accepts.
- Limited to single-family homes only. Most cash buyers refuse commercial, land, or multi-family. We buy everything.
